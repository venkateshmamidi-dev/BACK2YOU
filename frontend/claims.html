Claim Verification Process

  1. Submit Claim — Fill out the ownership verification form with as much detail as possible.
  2. Admin Review — A campus admin reviews your answers and the AI confidence score.
  3. Verification — Admin may ask for additional proof (receipt, ID, etc).
  4. Handover — Once approved, admin coordinates the item handover with the finder.
  5. Finder Rewarded — The finder receives Helper Points for their contribution.

Claim Tips

Be as specific as possible. Vague claims are more likely to be rejected.

Unique marks like stickers, scratches, or engravings are very strong identifiers.

False claims are taken seriously. Only submit a claim for items that are genuinely yours.
